## What I Do

**Technical Leadership**

- Translate business requirements into technical roadmaps and well-scoped deliverables

**Backend Engineering**
- Design and build APIs, distributed services and business-critical systems
- Deep hands-on experience with **Ruby on Rails**, **Node.js** and **Java/Kotlin with Spring Boot**

- Operating production systems with a focus on visibility, incident response and stability

- **Simplicity over cleverness** — systems and code that the whole team can understand and evolve
